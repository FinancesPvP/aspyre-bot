version: 1.8
- New ticket panel: pick a ticket type from a dropdown, like CavePvP's Support Center
- Ticket types: General Support, Player Report, Store Support, Punishment Appeal and Staff Application
- Each ticket is named by its type (report-name, store-name, appeal-name…) and tells the member exactly what to send first
- Each type can go into its own Discord category; anything without one goes into General
+ Server status panel: live player count and status for aspyre.cc, updated every minute
+ KOTH / Conquest / EOTW alerts: event start pings with a countdown, sent to an alert role
+ Warn system: /warn with a history log and automatic timeouts after repeated warnings
+ Invite tracking: see who invited each member and an invites leaderboard
+ Ticket ratings: members rate their support 1–5 stars after a ticket closes
+ Message logs: deleted and edited messages logged to a staff channel
