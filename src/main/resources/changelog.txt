version: 1.6
- Added `/ticket stats`: shows how many tickets each staff member has claimed and closed
- `/ticket stats` can show a single staff member (`member:`) or only the last N days (`days:`)
- Tickets now remember who closed them and when, so stats stay accurate
+ Ticket ratings: after a ticket closes, the member rates their support from 1–5 stars
+ Auto-close inactive tickets after 48 hours with no messages, with a warning first
+ Message logs: post deleted and edited messages to a staff log channel
+ Live member count: a locked voice channel that shows the server's member count
+ In-game punishment sync: bans and mutes on the Minecraft server show up in a Discord log channel
